01 / INTRODUCTION

A little about me.

Curiosity started it. Engineering keeps me building.

I'm Soren Shamlou, a software developer with an early background in electronics, hardware programming and robotics. I started experimenting with circuits and C programming, built robots, and achieved first place in a line-following robot competition.

Over time, my interests moved toward software engineering and web development. Today, I spend most of my time building backend applications, designing APIs, working with databases, and improving application workflows.

I also enjoy exploring Linux, application security, automation and the intersection of hardware and software. I value practical learning, clean implementation and systems that remain maintainable as they grow.
